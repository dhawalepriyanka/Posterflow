# Original signature templates

These are new PosterFlow compositions inspired by the requested categories and personalisation workflow, not reproductions of Leadr screenshots. Existing templates and screens are preserved.

- Birthday → Midnight Celebration: burgundy, gold lights, serif heading, framed portrait.
- Festival → Divine Craft: original devotional artwork, Hindi greeting, small portrait and branded footer.
- Motivation → Rise Every Day: ivory and deep teal, warm orange accents, editable quote and portrait.

Open Templates, choose the category and design, upload a photo, edit the name/message, and save or share. Company/logo/contact details use the existing Profile settings. Pinch and drag to adjust the photo. These additions target the image-template gallery; the existing video collection is unchanged. Output remains the app's 1080 × 1920 portrait format. Photos use a frame; automatic background removal is not included.

Artwork: `app/src/main/res/drawable/studio_vishwakarma.png`.
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Typography, portrait frames, footer and the other two backgrounds are drawn by the app, so user details remain editable. No reference logos, names or portraits were copied.

No text, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no border, no modern human portraits. Respectful ornate devotional illustration of Lord Vishwakarma as an elderly white-bearded divine craftsman wearing a gold crown, warm saffron and golden robes, seated on a gilded throne with traditional tools and a luminous ornamental circular halo. Place the complete deity and throne in the right 52 percent of the canvas, occupying upper/middle 78 percent. Rich magenta-to-indigo dusk gradient atmosphere, subtle temple silhouettes along lower horizon, a few tasteful marigold decorations at the very top. Left 43 percent must remain uncluttered deep purple negative space for editable Hindi greeting text, bottom 18 percent simple dark violet for later editable company footer and a portrait. High detail, tasteful luminous gold, polished traditional Indian festival greeting illustration. Output only the artwork, not a phone mockup or app screenshot.
